One small control, three clear steps

From stream to finished take.

Start the helper from the menu bar. Rippa handles the setup sequence, then gets out of the way while you listen.

01 / PREPARE

Get Reason ready

Start Recording opens your configured Reason project, selects the audio route, prepares the session, and shows a clear READY state.

02 / RECORD

Play what you love

Rippa can wait for playback to begin, then keep recording in sync. Pause or resume recording and playback together with ⌥⌘R.

03 / FINISH

Loop and export

Stop & Finish wraps the selected take and runs Reason’s audio export. Optional local Ollama naming can use the tracks; otherwise Rippa uses a timestamp name.

Before your first take

A little audio setup.

Rippa coordinates the tools already on your Mac; it isn’t a replacement for the DAW or an isolated per-app audio recorder.

What you’ll need

  • macOS and Reason 10.4
  • A virtual loopback input configured in Reason
  • A configured macOS aggregate audio output
  • switchaudio-osx and nowplaying-cli helpers
  • Accessibility and Automation permissions for control

Know how the audio route works

Rippa records system audio routed through your configured aggregate output into Reason’s selected input. It does not capture an isolated audio stream from one app.

Ollama is optional and runs locally for track-based names; if it isn’t available, Rippa falls back to a timestamp name. Launching Rippa alone does not start recording or change audio settings.
